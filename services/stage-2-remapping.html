Stage 2 remapping

How it works.

Stage 2 is tuned around the parts you have fitted, such as an uprated intercooler, intake, downpipe or exhaust. We check what's on the car first, then write a map that matches it.

  • Tuned to your exact hardware list
  • Bigger gains than Stage 1
  • Gearbox (TCU) tuning available to match
  • Priced per car once we know the setup

Prices vary with the make, model, engine and ECU type. Send your reg and we'll confirm the method and a fixed price before booking.
